How to play
  • 1–7 pick a tower, then click the grass to build it
  • Hold Shift while clicking to build several of the same tower
  • Click a tower on the map to see its range or sell it
  • R or the big blue button starts a round; press it again to speed up
  • P pause / resume · Space one tick · F change speed
  • A toggle auto rounds · Esc cancel · Del sell selected
